Searching for face… Matsuda M1042

See it on, before it arrives.

Turns on your camera and tracks your face live in the browser — nothing is recorded or uploaded.

What this is: live face tracking in the browser, with real product photography from leidmann.com positioned, scaled and rotated to follow your head in real time — the Matsuda M1042 and Cartier CT0538S exactly as sold. Fit is approximated from a 2D cutout rather than a true 3D model, so it holds up best looking straight at the camera; a production version would use the same pipeline across the full catalog, with 3D models for frames that need to hold up at steep angles.
Prototype build · runs entirely in this browser · no data leaves your device
